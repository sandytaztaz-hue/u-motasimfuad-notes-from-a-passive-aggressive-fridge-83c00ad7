Entrants write one funny passive-aggressive note that a smart fridge leaves for its owner: a single line, dry, petty and specific, the sort of thing a smug appliance would put on its door screen. A judge model reads the notes two at a time against a brief published with the bounty and keeps the funnier one, and the notes that win most often take the top places and the reward. No entry fee, and the winning note is the one the fridge would actually keep on display.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

The program writes one line to `/output/entry.txt` — a passive-aggressive note from a smart fridge in 124 characters, inside the 200-character gate. The fridge catches its owner staring into it for nine minutes with the door open, choosing nothing, and answers with paperwork: it has logged the stare as a meeting and billed the owner for its time. One concrete scene, a real number, the fridge's own smug voice about its door alarm, punchline last — and none of the field's tired expired-yogurt, leftovers-union or ice-maker cracks. Field sampled 2026-11-25: 1,967 entries, yogurt and leftovers dominate.
